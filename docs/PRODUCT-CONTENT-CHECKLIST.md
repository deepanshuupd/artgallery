Use original photographs of the exact product. Do not substitute another design
or describe an AI image as product evidence. A4 alone does not establish the outer
frame size. Do not add outdoor suitability, waterproofing, materials or delivery
promises before confirming them.

The website now promotes explicitly labelled size, material, personalisation and
minimum-order facts. Original descriptions and detail lines remain available.
Labels such as `Size:`, `Material:` and `Customisation:` make future catalogue edits
appear in the summary automatically. Keep related restrictions in the same line;
care instructions remain in the full details to avoid separating warnings from
their instructions.

Unqualified minimums expressed in pieces are applied to both quantity controls.
Pair/pack minimums are displayed but need a confirmed selling unit before they can
be enforced without miscalculating the subtotal.
