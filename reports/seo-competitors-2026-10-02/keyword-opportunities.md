# KumaonRang: 70 proposed keyword targets

Prepared 2 October 2026. This is an **editorially prioritised target list**, not a list of queries competitors demonstrably rank for. Ordering reflects product relevance, specificity and the usefulness of the existing destination. It does not reflect measured rankability, demand or expected traffic.

No search volume, keyword-difficulty score, Google position, traffic estimate or ranking promise is supplied. Competition labels are **unmeasured hypotheses**. The supporting competitor research is in [competitors.md](competitors.md); product relevance comes from user-confirmed inventory and the [historical catalogue audit](../seo-mobile/seo-audit.json). The main audit task confirmed that the live sitemap returned HTTP 200 and listed 58 URLs, including the product paths used here. This does not guarantee stock, delivery capacity or a fresh crawl/indexing check for each page.

## How to use the list

- **Observed page phrase** means the wording, or a short contiguous stem, appeared in a cited competitor page. Capitalisation may be normalised. It does not mean the phrase is a measured query.
- **Derived variant** means an editorial target assembled from confirmed inventory, competitor terminology and purchase intent. Added words such as “online,” occasion wording and product-specific combinations are hypotheses to validate with the site's own search data.
- **I** means inventory evidence. A competitor source alongside I supplies category context, not proof that the competitor sells that precise design. In particular, the Golu Devta, jewellery-display and acrylic-magnet targets originate in KumaonRang's inventory.
- **Narrower opportunity** means a specific product/design combination may have fewer directly substitutable pages. **Specialist competitive** means relevant niche merchants demonstrably cover the product family. **Broad likely harder** means a less specific term plausibly spans more merchants and intents. None is a measured competition finding; narrower wording can also have little or no demand.
- Each destination link is an existing URL selected as the preferred canonical destination for that intent. This mapping does not assert that canonical tags have been independently rechecked in this research file. Related spellings and singular/plural variants share a page. No new doorway routes are proposed.

## Mapping and differentiation rules

The Aipankari former-reseller relationship is user-supplied, not independently verified. It supports a practical emphasis on KumaonRang's own maker story, original photographs, exact product facts and direct customisation process. It does not justify copying descriptions, alleging infringement, or claiming exclusive ownership of a traditional art form. State which items KumaonRang makes and which it curates or sources.

The two Om products, two Ganesh products, several couple keychains, girl-keychain variants and acrylic magnets can have legitimate differences. This list selects a primary destination for each proposed wording; it does not direct redirects, canonical changes, product merging or removal. Confirm the real distinctions before implementation. If later evidence shows duplicate listings, handle that through a separate catalogue/canonical review.

Keep spelling alternatives such as Ganesh/Ganesha, personalised/personalized, customised/customized, and nameplate/name plate on the same intended page. Preserve the existing URLs with historical misspellings such as `jwellery` and `arcylic` unless an explicit migration includes redirects and link updates; the visible copy can use correct spelling.

Treat the Pichwai combo as its actual giftware/art style. Do not call it Aipan or imply that every component is handmade in Pithoragarh. The unqualified birthday/wedding hamper phrases refer to the confirmed hamper category; use occasion wording only where the displayed contents and options support that purchase.

Do not expand this implementation into unconfirmed Kedarnath, Trishul, Swastik, Lakshmi Charan, Saraswati, exact cow/peacock jar variants, corporate bulk promises or unsupported materials. Do not create one page per phrase. Publish coherent product and collection copy, then use Search Console query-by-page evidence to refine priorities, detect mismatched intent and decide whether further content is warranted.
