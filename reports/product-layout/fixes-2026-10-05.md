# Product layout corrections — 5 October 2026

Implemented locally in `src/components/products/product-detail-view.tsx` and `product-detail.module.css`.

## Changes

- Mobile photographs come first. A square photo well uses `object-fit: contain`; the original photographs are unchanged. The thumbnail strip uses an explicit grid track instead of depending on its intrinsic flex width. Four complete thumbnail buttons fit at 320, 390 and 430px phone widths.
- Product titles use 26px at 320/390px, about 28px at 430px and 30px on tablet. Desktop titles range from 30 to 36px. The price and introduction also use a restrained scale.
- The title, price, availability, enquiry actions and details form one desktop panel, starting at the same height as the gallery. Desktop gallery height adapts to the viewport, and the gallery stays below the navigation while scrolling.
- Accessible disclosure buttons use `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and labelled content regions. Closed content is inert. One section opens at a time, with a 280ms height-grid transition and 220ms opacity transition. Reduced-motion CSS removes these transitions.
- The information area reserves 252px on mobile and 260px on desktop. Longer descriptions/specifications scroll within that area. This keeps the card and the following sections in place when a section opens.
