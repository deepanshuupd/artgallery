# Aipan guide expansion — 15 October 2026

The existing `/aipan-art` guide now covers history and names, geru and bisvar, the traditional process, nine design groups, five motif groups, ceremonial settings, GI recognition, buying advice and nine reader questions. The article remains on its existing canonical URL. Its former `/pithoragarh-aipan-art` address retains the permanent redirect.

## Research and editorial choices

The public guide contains numbered references linked to eleven sources. Government craft documentation, NID Bengaluru’s D’source resource, Project FUEL’s collaboration with UHHDC, the official GI Registry, Sangeet Natak Akademi’s hosted documentation and a G.B. Pant University research paper form the primary evidence. eUttaranchal provides attributed regional context for named ceremonial designs.

- [GI Registry, application 648](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/648): use the certificate date, 14 September 2021; do not infer that KumaonRang or its catalogue is an authorised GI user.
- [NID: art forms](https://www.dsource.in/resource/aipan-uttarakhand/art-forms) and [regional design descriptions](https://www.euttaranchal.com/culture/aipan-other-arts-form.php): distinguish named ritual designs from a decorative product category. The design list is illustrative, not exhaustive.
- [NID: process](https://www.dsource.in/resource/aipan-uttarakhand/designing-process) and [Project FUEL/UHHDC](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/pwUB1enKByW2Vg): rice preparation and finger technique vary in the accounts; avoid presenting one recipe as universal.
- [Asha Upreti’s documentation](https://indiaich-sna.in/sites/default/files/2023-11/report_26.pdf): provides regional and household context. Its broader classification includes related Kumaoni art forms; clay images are not floor paintings.
- [2020 university research](https://ijcmas.com/9-10-2020/Manisha%20Gahlot,%20et%20al.pdf): supports explaining the difference between a ritual centre and decorative adaptation.

Do not invent an exact age, a universal motif count, spiritual guarantees, stock availability or product materials. Keep the supplied artwork’s existing attribution and descriptive alt text. No external illustration is copied or presented as a verified ritual diagram.

## Search intent and implementation

| Reader’s question | Relevant content |
| --- | --- |
| What is Aipan art of Uttarakhand? | Definition, region, history, related spellings |
| What are the types of Aipan? | Named chowkis, peeths, thapa and patta, each with context |
| How is Aipan made? | Geru, bisvar/biswar, fingers and preparation steps |
| What do Aipan motifs mean? | Dots, footprints, lotus and geometric/religious symbols |
| What Aipan is used at Diwali or weddings? | Occasion explanations and links to named designs |
| How do I choose Aipan frames or gifts? | Catalogue links, materials distinction and buying advice |

These terms reflect the researched subject, not measured search volumes. The page uses a descriptive title, summary and H1, a visible organization byline and revision date, Article metadata matching the visible content, source citations and section links. It adds no guide-specific client component, image download, dependency or animation. No FAQ rich-result eligibility or ranking improvement is promised. [Google’s content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports useful, original explanations rather than repetition aimed at rankings.
